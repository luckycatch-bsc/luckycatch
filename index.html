§6The Pond — where the 50 BNB waits

When the mint window closes, this door opens. Every Lucky Catch you hold becomes a rod cast into a pond holding 50 BNB, put there by the club. Catches are not equal: a Silver Minnow fishes with minnow odds; a Golden Koi fishes like it owns the place. The exact weights are published before the pond opens — and the order never changes: Common < Rare < Epic < Legendary.

§8Clubhouse questions

What does the free post have to say?

Use the ready template behind the ① button — it carries #LuckyCatch, the words 50 BNB, and this site's address. The gate reads the post to check. For Box B, the posting account also needs its blue check.

Can I claim from both free boxes?

Yes. One free catch per wallet per box — plenty of members take both. A single X account, though, can only power one free catch in total.

Is there really no buy limit?

Really. Buy 1 or buy 400; the contract does not count per person or per purchase. The only ceiling is the 2,500 in the paid box.

When does the pond open?

The moment the 3-day mint ends. The button in §6 unlocks then, and the fishing weights are published before it opens.

Where does the 50 BNB come from?

The club puts it in when the pond opens, funded separately. It is not skimmed from mint money — paid mint money goes to the treasury, and the pond is its own purse.

Which wallet and chain?

BNB Chain (chain 56), through WalletConnect or any browser wallet. Permits from the gate are always free — never pay anyone for one.

Can the club change the rules later?

No. The contract has no admin functions at all. The caps, the price and the window were fixed the day it was deployed.
